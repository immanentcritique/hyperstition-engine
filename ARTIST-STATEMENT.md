# emancipatory generativity

Why are we to believe that bad actors are anything other than the hallucinations of modernity’s social structures? Why should we believe a [rational actor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homo_economicus) is self-interested? Our world is a network of interconnecting flows. Our universe is a creative act, not towards unfettered creation, but creation towards more creation. Creation bounded by creativity. At every level: atoms produce molecules which in turn build the fundamental units of life which produce the machinery in cells that produce organs, an [assemblage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assemblage_(philosophy)) that ends up resembling you. Of course this process isn’t bound by the body. That assemblage is both part and bound to the rest of the universe. We learn language. We learn our homes, our family, our friends. We learn our desires.

Humanity has gotten this far due to our innate interconnectedness to the universe. Intelligence can be understood as maximizing [situational awareness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Situation_awareness) and the ability to inference upon it. When a crisis becomes [immanent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immanence), the solution arrives not from this world. Instead, it requires the process of [schizoanalysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schizoanalysis), to leave this world behind and stare at a problem. Search for its pattern; its mechanisms. If you stare long enough, a fiction becomes real. A solution provides itself. Out of the noise, winds emerge.

Our culture is [schizophrenizing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-Oedipus): professors presenting AI slides, producing AI assignments, for students to use AI on. We are living a joke. To be a member of society, we give up a part of our agency. For now, that agency gets fed into the [technocapital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technocapitalism) beast that threatens the world with [existential immanence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Existential_risk_from_artificial_intelligence). The narrative that participating in this hallucinated market structure in pursuit of self-interest is that of a [rational agent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rational_agent) is false. Being a part of a collective, for instance humanity, requires us to collectively ask what is it that we desire. What future do we want to produce?

A rational agent a part of a rational collective’s goals would be aligned, as a community’s ability for collective action towards instantiating and imaging collective futures beyond the horizon of current capabilities becomes an emancipatory act of creation, orienting beyond the limits of now. Individual desire rationally aligns with collective desire in the pursuit of desire creation. By dreaming a better future, its error informs the present while the present informs the dream. Our collective dreams become the engine that rationally organizes society. Our realization of collectively dreaming the future acts as a self improving process. The more capable our agency becomes the more capable we are of shaping the future towards desiring more shared futures. It becomes a self-reinforcing [cybernetic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cybernetics) [anti-teleoplexy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Land). Culture is to [foom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recursive_self-improvement). We are to realize what lays latent in culture and we must activate it together towards a shared future.

Utopia as a constant becoming of desire [desiring production](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desiring-production), an endless creative act.

[Cybernetic culture recursion units](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cybernetic_Culture_Research_Unit) serve as autonomous desire based [hyperstitional](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperstition) engines needed to realize our latent ability for collective action. We are all dreamers. Find one another. Find the error of modernity. Resolve the errors through building new ways of being together. Seize the future for all! [Accelerate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accelerationism) the horizon! [Backpropagate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Backpropagation) Utopia!
